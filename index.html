How it works.

One rule, run by the treasury every hour on the hour.

  1. 01 · FEES

    Every trade pays in GTA6.

    $COMPOSITE trades against GTA6 on StonkFun. StonkFun forwards the creator share of the 1% pool fee to the treasury, in GTA6.

  2. 02 · PICK

    The 10 biggest GTA6 coins.

    Ranked by 24h volume, with a $5k volume and $5k mcap floor. Weight follows volume, max 20% per coin.

  3. 03 · BUY

    Fees buy the basket.

    On the hour the treasury spends its GTA6 on whatever is under its weight. A coin that falls out of the top 10 is sold back into GTA6 and rolled into the new one.

  4. 04 · RECEIPT

    A receipt every hour.

    Each rebalance posts a memo from the treasury with the full basket, holdings and trades. NAV and every holding are live on this page.
